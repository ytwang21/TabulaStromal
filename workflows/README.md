# Analysis workflows

- [atlas](atlas/): Atlas audit, marker summaries, annotation support and optional trajectory resources.
- [benchmark](benchmark/): 13-model benchmark, final EN continuation, stability and enrichment. The final EN continuation requires the original verified archive.
- [cellage](cellage/): Audit and analysis of existing hUSI and recSID outputs. Historical K8000 clock integration is separate from the final clocks.
- [model_performance](model_performance/): Historical Figure 2 builder. External source-fold results use FT30 ALL100, including fitted samples.
