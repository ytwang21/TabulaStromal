# Figure Legends

Figure numbers below are manuscript assignments. The manifest maps every assigned panel to an unchanged source image and its supporting table. Figures 3–5 are collections of existing panels, presented with assigned panel letters in the combined Word previews; the source images have not been relabeled or statistically reprocessed. Error bars, colors and thresholds describe each named source panel rather than imposing a shared convention across unrelated analyses.

## Figure 1. Stromal reference states and metadata coverage

(A,B) Original UMAPs colored by broad fibroblast and endothelial-lineage annotation, respectively. Each displays 160,000 stratified representative cells; full-source collections contain 5,916,760/2,239,277 cells and 4,818/3,553 sample labels. (C,D) The same plotted cells colored by the final Celltype-label suffix, comprising twelve/eleven categories including pan. These categories are not original anatomical tissue. (E) Full-cell and distinct-sample counts by suffix category; leftward fibroblast bars and rightward endothelial bars both encode positive counts, and a sample may contribute to several categories. (F) Counts by stored Age_group, including unknown ages. (G,H) Marker dot plots: point area encodes detection fraction and blue intensity encodes gene-wise min–max-scaled mean stored expression. Unavailable fibroblast markers PI16, LUM and IL33 were omitted. (I,J) Broad-state signature means: colors are column z-scores and printed values are unscaled means. Continuous expression colors differ from annotation colors. Counts and expression summaries use full-source cells; there are no inferential error bars. Broad-state support is descriptive and shares the annotated source data. The endothelial-lineage collection retains its source MCs category. Source version: fig1_20261103_v2.

## Figure 2. Clock overview, source-fold benchmark and current-model development fits

(A) Supplied workflow with updated performance-first source-model and external prediction elements. The actual developmental model fits use 750/350 features in 640/437 fibroblast/endothelial samples, with R² 0.950/0.950, MAE 4.98/5.03 years and MedAE 3.66/3.50 years. Lower external panels use adapted ALL100 predictions in 93/77 samples, with R² 0.377/0.391 and MAE 8.71/9.28 years. External outcomes informed model selection, so these values are not independent validation. Other workflow elements remain conceptual artwork: illustrative correlation/P values, donor icons, the healthy-only label and GWAS sketches do not establish matched empirical results. The target is recorded chronological age, and independent donor identity is unresolved.

(B–D) Source-fold-4 R², MAE and Age_MAD for thirteen models in training, validation and FT30→ALL100 external sets. Feature count was chosen per model using that fold's validation result. These panels are not five-fold averages and do not evaluate the current compact models. R² colors are centered on zero; larger MAE is poorer error performance. Panel D MAD = mean(|Age − mean(Age)|), a label-dispersion metric. External evaluation includes adaptation samples. ElasticNet highlighting indicates the interpretation branch rather than global superiority. (E,F) Current performance-first source-model predictions on the same development samples used for fitting; one point per sample, all eligible points retained, with chronological age on the x-axis and predicted age on the y-axis. Dashed identity lines distinguish perfect prediction. R² 0.950/0.950, MAE 4.98/5.03 and MAD 3.66/3.50 describe in-sample fit. Here and in the updated model elements of A, MAD means median absolute prediction error (MedAE), unlike panel D. Negative predictions were not clipped. There are no inferential performance error bars. Source: Fig2_StromalClock_ModelPerformance.png and its current data/source manifest.

## Figure 3. Current selected features and lineage-associated functional annotations

(A,B) Counts of exact selected Celltype_Gene features by fibroblast/endothelial state; colors identify the broad subtype. Counts sum to 750/350 features, encoding 678/337 genes across twenty-seven states per lineage. Bars are model feature counts rather than cell abundance or independently tested aging importance. (C,D) Source-defined top dynamic selected-feature displays. Rows are scaled across original A–H age groups; display filtering requires coverage and dynamic range, and up to thirty features are shown. A–C, D–F and G–H form display windows without asserting equal chronological intervals. Neither heatmap selection nor color is a differential-expression test.

(E,F) Fibroblast all-selected-gene Hallmark and GO cellular-component enrichment. (G,H) Endothelial Reactome 2022 and GO cellular-component enrichment. In these dot plots, the x-axis gives the fraction of query genes overlapping each term, point area increases with overlap count, and color encodes −log₁₀ BH FDR; FDR is library-specific BH across the complete eligible term family, including zero-overlap terms. Backgrounds comprise 11,700/11,521 measured genes. (I,J) Corresponding gene–functional-theme networks. Edges encode membership in significant annotated themes, with display restrictions on overlap and edge count. They are not gene-regulatory or physical interactions. Coefficient-derived signs and weights describe conditional prediction, not gene activation or causal aging direction. The ALL100-informed feature selection makes all downstream interpretation exploratory. Original panel paths and input tables are provided in figure_manifest.csv.

## Figure 4. Public chromatin-based regulator inference and prior-constrained RNA support

(A) LISA2 eligibility among the twenty-seven selected states per lineage: fourteen fibroblast and five endothelial states completed; other states lacked sufficient mapped genes. Missing states are not negative findings. (B,C) LISA2 factor rankings/heatmaps for eligible fibroblast/endothelial states. Color encodes −log₁₀ lineage-wide BH FDR (capped at fifteen); the main text uses the same lineage-wide factor-by-state family. Factor P values account for the number of ChIP datasets before BH correction. LISA2 uses public chromatin and regulatory-potential information and does not measure factor activity in the present cells. (D,E) Prior-constrained RNA-network heatmaps for selected-feature targets in fibroblasts/endothelial cells. The source networks retained sixty-five/seven selected-target edges under the recorded partial-correlation, sign and bootstrap-support criteria. Color encodes the number of supported selected target genes per factor–state pair; it does not encode count-based age fold changes. (F,G) Example eFibro_COL1A1_pan and EndoMT_VIM_pan candidate networks. Edges combine CollecTRI prior knowledge with RNA support and do not establish direct binding, enhancer grammar or synergistic regulation. Unavailable nodes or edges were not imputed. The stricter adult factor-age sensitivity produced no lineage-wide FDR-significant association; the displayed whole-age network is not evidence of general adult age regulation. No common error-bar convention applies to these descriptive network panels.

## Figure 5. Matrix-associated adult age contrasts differ by cellular and tissue context

(A,B) Selected-gene membership in ECM signatures for fibroblasts/endothelial cells. Membership alone does not establish an age effect. (C,D) ECM-signature age-contrast scores calculated from adult raw-count negative-binomial Wald statistics in eligible cell-state or cell-state-by-tissue profiles. Positive/negative scores indicate association with higher/lower older-versus-younger expression statistics under the signed gene-set model. Color encodes ULM scores capped at ±3; point area encodes −log₁₀ P capped at five, black outlines identify FDR ≤ 0.05, and gray crosses indicate unavailable scores or fewer than five mapped targets. The primary younger/older threshold is the tissue-specific median eligible training-proxy age capped at sixty years, not a fixed ≤50/≥60 split. Significance follows BH across all 312 ECM comparisons from both lineages; overlapping signatures and scopes are dependent. (E) Endothelial mechanosensitive-factor display after adjustment across the complete 22,346-factor-test family. One of 142 displayed mechanosensitive comparisons is significant: TEAD4 in adipose ArtEC_FLT1_pan, q = 0.0174, twelve sample proxies from one study. Gene-set regression scores are not directly measured protein activity.

(F,G) Globally selected ECM-associated gene-age heatmaps across fibroblast/endothelial cell-state profiles. Color denotes raw-count Wald z, capped at ±4, for up to thirty-five genes selected by maximum absolute statistic. These panels use cell-state-level profiles, can show genes outside their original selected Celltype_Gene identity and do not display every tissue-specific contrast. Exact-feature tissue-specific results are reported separately in Table 2, including PLA2G4A in ovary eFibro_COL1A1_pan (log₂ fold change 2.60; full gene-by-profile q = 0.0132). Profiles can share samples, and no formal cross-tissue interaction or broad activation mechanism is inferred. Raw-count sums retain observed values; noninteger genes were excluded per profile rather than rounded. Source coverage tables retain sample numbers and unavailable fits.

## Figure 6. Senescence-associated transcription in the current selected states

The original ModelFeature_CellAge composite supplies all panels, with fibroblast on the left and endothelial on the right in each paired row. (A,B) State-specific relative hUSI, defined as the paired mean-score difference from other cell-weighted lineage cells in the same sample-by-tissue unit. (C,D) Adjusted within-state hUSI change per ten years. Forest intervals are 95% t-based confidence intervals with GSE-clustered uncertainty for at least five studies or HC3 uncertainty for limited-study fits. Orange marks source BH FDR ≤ 0.05; unavailable estimates are labeled NA. Primary score aggregation requires twenty scored cells and at least 95% coverage. (E,F) Mean sample-level hUSI across original A–H labels, displayed after within-state row standardization using the sample standard deviation, with color capped at ±2 z-score units. (G,H) Mean sample-level state proportions (%) across stored age groups. Gray heatmap entries are unavailable. These grouped summaries do not create additional independent samples.

(I,J) Adjusted older-minus-younger state proportions in percentage points, requiring at least one hundred lineage cells per unit. The binary comparison uses source B/C versus F/G/H labels, with D/E omitted from that comparison only. (K,L) Exploratory relationships between model feature count/importance and CellAge summaries. Primary FDR families are separate for six metrics and include estimable tests from both lineages; exploratory correlations use their full saved family. None of the primary age-dependent hUSI, SSE or composition metrics, or exploratory importance correlations, survives its applicable correction. Native hUSI values reflect senescence-associated transcription, not age in years or age acceleration. Current selected genes overlap extensively with the hUSI signature, limiting independence. Source-specific study and sample-proxy counts are retained in the tables.
